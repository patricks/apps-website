Streak Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Streak processes your workout information on your device and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Controller and contact

Streak is provided by Patrick Steiner. For privacy questions or to exercise your data-protection rights, contact streak@helmsdeep.at.

Health data

With your permission, Streak reads workout information from Apple Health through HealthKit to show your activity, progress, statistics, and running goals. This information is handled on your device and is not sent to Patrick Steiner or to a third-party server by Streak. Streak does not sell, use for advertising, or disclose HealthKit data.

You can change or revoke Streak’s Health access at any time in the Health app or your device settings. Apple Health manages HealthKit data separately from Streak.

Data stored on your device

Streak stores settings and locally derived progress information on your device. Your weekly distance goal is also synchronised through Apple iCloud across your devices. Streak does not require a separate account with Patrick Steiner.

For troubleshooting, you can choose to export diagnostic logs and share them using the system share sheet. Logs may contain technical information about the app’s operation.

Third-party services and purchases

Streak does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If the app offers purchases, payment processing is provided by Apple; Patrick Steiner does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Privacy page hosting

This privacy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. No additional analytics or advertising scripts are used on this page. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact requests

If you email us, we process the information in your message only to respond to your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries or, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We will publish changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

Weightbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Weightbot handles your weight information on your device and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Controller and contact

Weightbot is provided by Patrick Steiner. For privacy questions or to exercise your data-protection rights, contact weightbot@helmsdeep.at.

Health data

With your permission, Weightbot may read and write weight and waist-circumference data in Apple Health through HealthKit. This information is handled on your device and is not sent to Patrick Steiner or to a third-party server by Weightbot. Weightbot does not sell, use for advertising, or disclose HealthKit data.

You can change or revoke Weightbot’s Health access at any time in the Health app or your device settings. Apple Health manages HealthKit data separately from Weightbot.

Data stored on your device

Weightbot stores its settings, including your preferred unit and reminder choices, on your device. Local notifications are used only when you enable reminders. The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit this information to Patrick Steiner.

Diagnostic logs

If you choose to upload diagnostic logs in the app, Weightbot sends logs from the previous 24 hours to Patrick Steiner’s diagnostic server for troubleshooting. These logs contain technical information about the app’s operation; the server also receives connection data such as your IP address. Uploading logs is optional.

Third-party services and purchases

Weightbot does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Optional tips and other purchases are processed by Apple; Patrick Steiner does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Privacy page hosting

This privacy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. No additional analytics or advertising scripts are used on this page. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact requests

If you email us, we process the information in your message only to respond to your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries or, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We will publish changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

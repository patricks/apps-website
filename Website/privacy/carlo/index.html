Carlo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Carlo is designed to keep your vehicle information under your control. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Controller and contact

Carlo is provided by Patrick Steiner. For privacy questions or to exercise your data-protection rights, contact carlo@helmsdeep.at.

Information handled by the app

Carlo stores vehicle details, fuel entries and cost records on your device and uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to sync them between your devices. Apple processes the synced data as part of its iCloud service. Carlo does not require a separate account with Patrick Steiner.

If you share a vehicle, Carlo uses CloudKit sharing to make the selected vehicle information available to the people you invite. Manage sharing through the app and Apple’s sharing controls.

Third-party services and purchases

Carlo does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If the app offers purchases, payment processing is provided by Apple; Patrick Steiner does not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Privacy page hosting

This privacy page is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. No additional analytics or advertising scripts are used on this page. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Contact requests

If you email us, we process the information in your message only to respond to your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries or, where relevant, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable requirements, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

Changes

We will publish changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
